Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

SeaBreeze Home Service LLC ("SeaBreeze," "we," "us") provides exterior home cleaning and lighting services in San Diego County, California. This policy explains what information we collect through www.seabreezehomeservice.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you request a quote, you fill out a form provided by Jobber, the scheduling and customer-management software we use. That form collects the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the work you need. We also receive whatever you share when you call, text, or email us.

Information collected automatically

Our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta Platforms, Inc. (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It uses cookies and similar technologies to record:

  • the pages you view on our site
  • whether you tap a phone, email, or quote-request link
  • technical details such as your IP address, browser, device type, and the website that referred you

Meta may combine this with information it already has about you, and may collect information about your activity across other websites over time.

When you submit a quote request, we send Meta a scrambled (hashed) copy of the email address and phone number you entered, along with the time of the request. Meta uses this only to tell us whether the request came from one of our ads. We never send Meta your name, address, or the details of your request.

Our hosting provider (Vercel) and font provider (Google Fonts) also receive standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads our pages.

How we use information

  • To respond to your quote request, schedule and perform services, and send invoices
  • To answer your questions
  • To understand how people find and use our website
  • To measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to quote requests, and to show our ads to people likely to be interested in our services

Who we share it with

We share information only with the companies that help us run our business: Jobber, to manage quote requests and jobs; Meta, for advertising measurement as described above; and our website hosting provider. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information for money.

Your choices

  • Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, our website does not load the Meta Pixel at all.
  • Quote requests. To keep your quote request from being shared with Meta, mention it in your request or contact us, and we will exclude it.
  • Do Not Track. Our website does not currently respond to the older Do Not Track browser setting. Use Global Privacy Control instead.
  • Ad settings. You can control how Meta uses your activity for ads in your Facebook ad preferences, and opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at aboutads.info.
  • Cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Our website still works without them.

Reviewing or correcting your information

To review, correct, or delete the information we hold about you as a customer, contact us at seabreezehomeservice@gmail.com or (619) 732-8052.

Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Security and retention

We keep customer information only as long as we need it to provide services, keep business records, and meet legal requirements. We rely on reputable service providers and reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
